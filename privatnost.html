Politika privatnosti

Alat „izbori“ pristupa samo Google Drive-u naloga koji ga pokreće i samo da bi čitao i pisao tabele i fajlove tog naloga. Podaci se ne dele sa trećim licima, ne prodaju se i ne čuvaju se van tog Google naloga i računara korisnika. Pristup se može opozvati u bilo kom trenutku na myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Privacy policy

The “izbori” tool accesses only the Google Drive of the account that runs it, solely to read and write that account's spreadsheets and files. Data is not shared with third parties, not sold, and not stored outside that Google account and the user's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
